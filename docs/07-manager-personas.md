# Manager Persona Examples

## Persona 1: People Manager

A people manager with 5 to 15 direct reports.

### Weekly pain

The manager moves between 1:1s, team meetings, recurring stakeholder updates, and unexpected follow-ups. Commitments are scattered across Teams, Outlook, and meetings.

### Copilot operating rhythm

| Cadence | Workflow | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Daily Manager Briefing | Shows people waiting, meetings needing prep, team commitments, and decisions |
| Before 1:1s | 1:1 Prep Assistant | Restores continuity from prior conversations and recent work context |
| 2 to 3 times per week | Team Commitments Review | Finds commitments, owners, blockers, and manager follow-ups |
| Weekly | Weekly Manager Review | Turns the week into outcomes, open loops, and next-week priorities |

### Success metric

The manager misses fewer follow-ups and reports better 1:1 continuity by day 30.

## Persona 2: Project Manager

A project or program manager coordinating delivery across teams.

### Weekly pain

Owners, dependencies, and dates move across channels. Risks emerge in meetings, Teams threads, email, and project files.

### Copilot operating rhythm

| Cadence | Workflow | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| 3 times per week | Team Commitments Review | Pulls commitments and owners into one view |
| Before delivery meetings | Meeting Prep Brief | Surfaces open decisions, context, and likely blockers |
| Weekly | Delivery Risk Watchlist | Finds evidence-backed risks and dependencies |
| Weekly | Weekly Manager Review | Produces a concise execution summary and next-week plan |

### Success metric

The project manager identifies blockers earlier and produces clearer owner follow-up by day 30.

## Persona 3: Functional Leader

A functional leader responsible for a department, business function, or cross-functional operating area.

### Weekly pain

The leader manages stakeholders, operating priorities, planning reviews, cross-team dependencies, and team-wide execution.

### Copilot operating rhythm

| Cadence | Workflow | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Daily Manager Briefing | Creates a short operating view across communications, calendar, and priorities |
| Weekly | Stakeholder Radar | Highlights unresolved asks and stakeholder follow-ups |
| Weekly | Focus and Calendar Protection | Finds low-value meetings, prep-heavy meetings, and focus blocks |
| Weekly | Weekly Manager Review | Converts the week into decisions, risks, outcomes, and next priorities |

### Success metric

The functional leader improves weekly priority alignment and reduces unresolved stakeholder asks by day 30.
