# Governance and Safety

## Operating rule

Start read-only.

Treat prompts and Agent Builder outputs as insight, summaries, drafts, and recommendations.

## Approval boundary

Require explicit approval before Copilot or an agent sends, deletes, declines, accepts, schedules, routes, creates tasks, updates records, changes documents, or modifies external systems.

## People safety

Do not use Copilot prompts or agents to score employee performance, rank employees, infer emotion, infer motivation, infer personality, assess attitude, compare employees, or make HR decisions.

Use observable work signals only.

Good:

> Alice committed to sending the QBR draft by Friday in the Teams thread.

Avoid:

> Alice seems disengaged.
